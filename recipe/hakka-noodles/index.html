Veg Hakka Noodles

Stir-fried noodles tossed with crunchy vegetables and soy sauce.

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • 🍽 3 servings
  • 📶 Easy

Ingredients (12)

  • 200 g hakka noodles
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin strips
  • 1 capsicum, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 3 spring onions, chopped (whites and greens kept apart)
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp chilli sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, to taste

Steps (5)

  1. Boil the noodles until just tender. Drain, rinse in cold water and toss with 1 tsp oil.
  2. Heat the rest of the oil in a wok on high heat and fry the garlic and spring onion whites for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the carrot, capsicum and cabbage, and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes so they stay crunchy.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce, vinegar, chilli sauce, pepper and salt.
  5. Toss everything on high heat for 2 minutes, then garnish with the spring onion greens.
