South Indian

Masala Dosa

Crispy rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potato masala.

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • 🍽 4 servings
  • 📶 Medium

Ingredients (9)

  • 2 cups dosa batter (fermented rice and urad dal)
  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8 curry leaves
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • Salt, to taste
  • 3 tbsp oil

Steps (6)

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan and add the mustard seeds. Let them splutter.
  2. Add the curry leaves, green chillies and onion, and cook until the onion is soft.
  3. Stir in the turmeric, potatoes and salt. Mash everything together and keep warm.
  4. Heat a flat pan, pour in a ladle of batter and spread it thinly in circles.
  5. Drizzle oil around the edge and cook until the dosa is golden and crisp.
  6. Spoon the potato masala into the centre, fold, and serve with chutney and sambar.
