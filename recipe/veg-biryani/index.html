Hyderabadi

Veg Biryani

Fragrant basmati rice layered with vegetables, saffron and mint.

  • ⏱ 60 min
  • 🍽 5 servings
  • 📶 Hard

Ingredients (11)

  • 1½ cups basmati rice, soaked for 30 minutes
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato)
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • A handful of mint and coriander leaves
  • Whole spices: 1 bay leaf, 4 cloves, 3 cardamom pods
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • Salt, to taste

Steps (6)

  1. Boil the rice with the whole spices and salt until it is about 70% cooked, then drain.
  2. Fry the onion in ghee until golden. Keep half aside for the topping.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste, vegetables, yogurt, biryani masala and salt. Cook for 8 minutes.
  4. Spread the rice over the vegetables. Top with the fried onion, mint, coriander and saffron milk.
  5. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat (dum) for 20 minutes.
  6. Rest for 5 minutes, fluff gently and serve with raita.
